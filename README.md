Hi, I am Amin Aminimehr, a Ph.D. student in Business Analytics at the University of Cincinnati with a research focus at the intersection of empirical asset pricing, econometrics, and machine learning.

My work centers on developing and applying statistical learning methods to financial markets, particularly in areas such as:

* Asset pricing
* Financial time series forecasting
* Regime detection
* Market efficiency
* Deep learning methods for financial data

I am particularly interested in understanding how modern machine learning architectures can be used not only for prediction, but also for uncovering dynamic structures in financial markets.

My technical work primarily involves Python-based research pipelines using tools such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, NumPy, pandas, and scikit-learn, alongside econometric and statistical modeling in R and MATLAB.

In addition to financial applications, I have contributed to projects involving traffic time-series forecasting and computer vision analysis of aerial traffic imagery.
